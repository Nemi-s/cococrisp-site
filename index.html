The method

From coconut to crisp.

i.

Pick & peel

Mature coconuts are opened and every trace of brown skin is pared away by hand.

ii.

Slice thin

The white flesh is cut into long, paper-thin ribbons so each chip bakes light and even.

iii.

Flavour

Slices are seasoned with salt or our spice blends, or rest in jaggery, to take on each flavour.

iv.

Bake & seal

Slow-dried and toasted until golden, then sealed straight away to keep the crunch.

FAQ

Good questions.

Are CocoCrisp chips fried?

No. They're slow-dried and toasted in an oven, never deep fried.

What are they made of?

Thin slices of real mature coconut, plus the seasoning for each variety: just salt for Classic Salted; chilli, amchur, black salt and chaat masala for Masala; chilli and lime for Chilli Lime; or jaggery and cardamom for Jaggery Elaichi. The full ingredient list is printed on every pack.

How much does a pack cost?

Each 50 g pack is ₹49 at our launch price. You'll see the full total, including any shipping, before you pay.

Where do you deliver?

We're starting in Mumbai. Not sure if we reach you? Contact us on WhatsApp from the link at the bottom of the page.

Which flavour should I try first?

If you like your chips namkeen, start with Classic Salted, then go bolder with Masala or Chilli Lime. Jaggery Elaichi is the one for a sweet tooth.

How should I store them?

Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place. Once opened, reseal it tightly so the chips stay crisp.
