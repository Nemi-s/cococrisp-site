How it's made

From coconut to crunch.

No shortcuts, just four careful steps.

Pick & peel

We choose mature coconuts, take out the white flesh and peel away every bit of brown skin.

Slice thin

The coconut is sliced paper-thin into long ribbons, so each chip turns light and crisp.

Flavour

The slices soak in jaggery, sugar syrup or our spice blends to take on each flavour.

Bake & pack

Slow-dried and toasted till golden, then sealed in pouches right away to lock in the crunch.

FAQ

Good questions.

Are CocoCrisp chips fried?

No. They're dried and toasted in an oven, never deep fried.

What are they made of?

Thin slices of real mature coconut, plus the flavouring for each variety: jaggery and cardamom, sugar syrup with spices, or chilli and lime. Full ingredient lists will be printed on every pack.

How do I order?

Tap "Add to cart" on any flavour, open the cart, then go to checkout and fill in your delivery details. Online payment is launching soon.

How much does a pack cost?

Each 50 g pack is ₹49 at our launch price. You'll see the full total, including any shipping, at checkout before you pay.

Where do you deliver?

We're starting in Mumbai. Not sure if we reach you? Contact us on WhatsApp from the link at the bottom of the page.

How should I store them?

Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place. Once opened, close it tightly or move the chips into an airtight box so they stay crunchy.
