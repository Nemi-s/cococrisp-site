About Classic Salted coconut chips

Classic Salted is the simplest CocoCrisp: mature coconut, pared of its brown skin, sliced paper-thin, seasoned with salt and baked until golden. There is no frying at any stage.

It is the one to start with if you are new to coconut chips: clean, crunchy and savoury, with the natural taste of toasted coconut.

How we make baked coconut chips

Pick & peel

Mature coconuts are opened and every trace of brown skin is pared away by hand.

Slice thin

The white flesh is cut into long, paper-thin ribbons so each chip bakes light and even.

Flavour

Slices are seasoned with salt or our spice blends, or rest in jaggery, to take on each flavour.

Bake & seal

Slow-dried and toasted until golden, never fried, then sealed straight away to keep the crunch.
